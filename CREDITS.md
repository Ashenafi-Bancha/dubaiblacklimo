# Image credits

Every photograph on the site is a **placeholder**, chosen so the site can be shown before the operator's own
photography is ready. Scenery is of Dubai and the UAE; the fleet shows the models offered. Replace the fleet photos
with pictures of the operator's real vehicles before launch.

The photos come from two free libraries:

- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use,
  attribution not required (credited here anyway).
- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), under the licence named on each row. **CC BY and CC BY-SA require
  attribution**: keep this file, or an equivalent credit on the site, for as long as those photos are used. CC BY-SA
  also requires that modified versions of those photos are shared under the same licence. CC0 has no conditions.

The originals are in `client/image-sources/`. To replace one, overwrite the file with your own photo (keep the file
name) and run `pnpm images`. Once a photo has been replaced, its row here can be removed.
